# Fleur de lis
## App Concept

*Fleur de lis* is a bouquet customization app built for people who want more control over how their flowers look instead of just relying on common shop displays. The primary users are individuals planning for special occasions such as birthdays, anniversaries, or valentines day, who often struggle to articulate their design preferences or imagine how different flowers will look together.  

Fleur de Lis addresses this need by allowing users to create and customize their own bouquet designs directly from their phones, whether they are at home, in a café, or planning an event elsewhere. With AI integration, the app can assist users in exploring bouquet ideas based on their preferences and help them visualize possible combinations. Once satisfied with their design, users can save and send their customized bouquet as a reference template to a florist. Unlike traditional flower-shopping experiences that offer limited preset arrangements, Fleur de Lis gives users a more personalized and interactive way to communicate their vision, making the process of designing and ordering flowers easier, more creative, and more personal.
